## T-1042 — Agent: Marcus

**Trigger Moment:**

**Coaching Goal:** Teach the agent how to acknowledge customer frustration, offer proactive assistance, and maintain control of the conversation to prevent escalation.

**Technique Demonstrated:** Empathy and proactive problem-solving: Recognizing the customer's feelings and offering concrete next steps to address their concerns, which helps to calm the situation and build trust.
